About Ballot Builder

A nonpartisan tool that helps you prepare for elections based on your own values — not party labels, endorsements, or someone else's opinion.

How It Works
1
You tell us what matters. A 5-minute quiz on 15 policy topics. You pick positions, not parties.
2
We score the candidates. Using voting records, interest group ratings, and stated positions — not party affiliation.
3
We compute your match. Topics you care more about count more. The result is a percentage.
4
You decide. We show evidence and reasoning. You pick your votes.
Where Candidate Scores Come From
PrioritySourceExample
HighestVoting recordsHow they voted on specific bills
HighestInterest group ratingsLCV, NRA, Chamber of Commerce scores
MediumCampaign websiteTheir own stated positions
MediumInterviewsPolicy answers in media Q&As
LowerNews & endorsementsReporting and org support
Your Privacy
All your data stays on your device. We don't send it to a server, track your positions, or share anything with anyone.
