Notes

  • A (Current) — Muted purple blends with the brand but feels washed out. The checkmark/number dots don't pop.
  • B (Slate) — Maximum neutrality. Lets the purple brand accent stand out in content below. Very "tool-like."
  • C (Navy) — Classic civic/government feel. Reads as trustworthy and nonpartisan. Strong contrast. My recommendation — it signals "civic tool" without any partisan color association.
  • D (Teal) — Fresh and modern. Clearly nonpartisan. Good contrast. But introduces a second accent color alongside the purple brand.
  • E (Warm Graphite) — Similar to slate but warmer. Feels premium/editorial. Pairs well with the purple accents below.
  • F (Deep Purple) — If you want to keep the purple family but make it richer and more confident. Better contrast than the current muted version.
