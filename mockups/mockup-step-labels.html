About You → What Matters → Your Picks

"Your Picks" implies agency — the system helps, the voter decides. Tradeoff: slightly more casual; "About You" is vague about ballot/location.

Option 4: Your Info / Your Priorities / Your Guide
Your Info → Your Priorities → Your Guide

"Info" is a natural bucket for location + demographics. "Priorities" signals values without sounding academic. "Guide" positions the output as helpful rather than prescriptive — you're getting a voting guide, not commands. Tradeoff: "Info" is generic, "Guide" is slightly vague about what you're getting.
